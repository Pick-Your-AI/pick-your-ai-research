# 3. Product Vision and T6 Scope

Pick Your AI is a PCPartPicker-style configuration platform for artificial intelligence.

Users select compatible models, infrastructure, knowledge systems, agents, training components, and operational services.

T6 determines whether the resulting configuration is:

- Safe and appropriately controlled.
- Measurable against workload requirements.
- Feasible to deploy.
- Observable in operation.
- Economically understandable.

## 5.2 Expert triage classification

Classify every action item as:

**Complete:** Acceptance satisfied and supported by merged, verifiable artifacts.

**In Progress:** Research or an accepted contribution is actively underway.

**Ready:** Work can begin without a genuine blocker.

**Needs input:** Meaningful progress requires a specific missing output from another issue; name that output. Merely helpful information does not prevent Ready status.

**Blocked:** A real prerequisite or permission prevents execution.

**Awaiting decision:** An owner decision is required.

**Duplicate:** Another canonical issue already tracks the same research deliverable.

**Out of Scope:** Belongs to a different workstream or the future implementation phase.

- Access controls.
