# Differentiation hypotheses and falsification

Status: not an accepted positioning decision. Evidence is in [profiles](competitors/README.md).

The broad claim that users lack model recommendation or hardware-fit tools is falsified by the reviewed product surfaces. A unique complete T0–T6 builder is neither established nor disproved: no exhaustive market proof or full end-to-end competing workflow test was performed.

| Hypothesis | Existing counterpressure | Falsification test | Current judgement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traceable compatibility advice saves more work than a directory | llmfit, ModelFit and workflow builders | Users finish a realistic task equally well with existing tools | Plausible, unvalidated |
| Workload-first cross-provider comparison has value | Flocci Ask/Stack Builder, Artificial Analysis and OpenRouter | Existing guided flow satisfies constraints and explanations without another tool | Strong counterexamples; prioritize direct testing |
| Versioned shared configuration evidence is a useful collaboration artifact | Existing model comparison and declarative deployment tools | Teams prefer their documents or deployment manifests | Plausible team use case; no buying evidence |
| Neutral cost advice differentiates | Provider aggregators and GPU comparison sites | Complete economics cannot be maintained cheaply or users distrust recommendations | High maintenance and incentive risk |

Potential defensibility is a reviewed, reproducible history of configuration outcomes and maintained rights-cleared evidence, not a larger scraped catalog. The cost of acquiring and maintaining that evidence may outweigh the moat. Do not use unmeasured completeness as marketing.
