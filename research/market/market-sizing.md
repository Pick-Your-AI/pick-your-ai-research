# Market boundaries, demand and sizing

Status: initial analysis, 2026-10-16; no revenue forecast.

The [Stanford 2026 AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/ai_index_report_2026.pdf) reports increased organizational AI adoption using survey evidence. Adoption is a broad enabling condition, not a count of buyers for configuration advice. The [2026 Stack Overflow survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/knowledge) gives evidence that source attribution matters to respondents. Neither source estimates willingness to pay for Pick Your AI.

Define the market by a recurring decision: selecting or revising a multi-component AI configuration. Exclude all general AI spend, model training capital and cloud revenue from the product's addressable-revenue denominator. Distinguish individual comparison users, team collaboration buyers and enterprise governance buyers; their purchase processes are different.

## Explicit scenarios, not market measurements

Annual subscription revenue = reachable qualified accounts × activation share × paying share × annual price. All inputs below are analyst assumptions in USD, not verified demand or proposed final prices.

| Scenario | Reachable accounts | Activation | Paid among active | Annual price | Annual gross subscription revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small validation channel | 1,000 | 10% | 5% | $120 | $600 |
| Focused developer channel | 10,000 | 20% | 5% | $120 | $12,000 |
| Team-oriented channel | 10,000 | 20% | 10% | $600 | $120,000 |

The large spread is the conclusion: distribution, activation, buyer identity and price dominate the estimate. These are neither TAM/SAM/SOM estimates nor evidence of attainable revenue. Exclude acquisition, churn, taxes, payment fees, support and data-license costs here; measure them before interpreting profitability.

## Demand and distribution gaps

Candidate channels are local-model communities, model/runtime integrations, developer search and consultancy referrals. Search results and competitor page counts demonstrate discoverability, not query volume or acquisition efficiency. No keyword-volume tool, Search Console property or ad experiment was available; absolute search demand and CAC remain unknown. No competitor revenue, verified active-user counts or concentration shares were established. Test a narrow landing proposition and measure qualified return use before expanding the catalog.
