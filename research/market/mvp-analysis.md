## Decision basis after #66

The [accepted substitute record at main 8dfec2d](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/8dfec2d58661232f5128f954cf2b5840450732a8/research/market/substitute-validation.md) supplies useful counterevidence: Flocci generated ordered advice and explicitly recognized the offline/hosted-API contradiction. Its localhost alternative was a disclosed relaxation, and the tested guest save preserved a tool bookmark rather than a reusable configuration. These findings remove guided advice and contradiction detection as sufficient differentiation; they do not establish whole-stack runtime/privacy feasibility or the global absence of exports. #66's [completion receipt](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/66#issuecomment-6248992055) releases the native prerequisite, without supplying this issue's separate receiving acceptance.

The [#57 decision](differentiation.md#decision-after-accepted-substitute-research-2026-10-18) narrows H1/H2 to claim-level traceability and explicit per-component alternatives, retains H3/H4/H5b as unvalidated questions and preserves the historical rejection of H5's broad single-model-history claim. Dify/n8n/SkyPilot export documentation supplies positive counterexamples to treating a portable record as inherently novel. The engineering choice is therefore to compare a minimal decision record against an existing format and README/manifest baseline before any broader build. This is analyst judgement under delegated project authority, not measured demand or a forecast.

The existing D-57-1–7 rules are adopted for their stated future experiments without changing their numerical thresholds or retroactively scoring #66. Workload-specific estimator error tolerances are deliberately not invented here: the selected initial record has no certified fit or savings claim. A later measured-fit claim must define its cohort, matched quantity and tolerance before collecting the relevant results with #10/#11; a complete-cost claim must first cover the selected meters with #39. The conditions in section 5 make those scope boundaries executable decisions rather than implicit passes.

## 2. Recommended next validation (conditional)

Validate a local-versus-API **single-user text-inference decision using public, non-sensitive inputs**, carrying explicit context/output reservations, concurrency, runtime, evidence date, uncertainty and a shareable versioned decision record. The local alternative is self-managed on one device; the API alternative identifies its operator and whether capacity is shared or dedicated. Before collecting a follow-up observation, pin the exact coherent workload inputs, alternatives, required fields, product/runtime versions and evaluator as required by D-57-1–3. Reuse source material only within #60's exact conditions. Retrieval, agents and fine-tuning remain optional future requirements; no stack-level claim is included. User value remains **HYP**.

The immediate comparison concerns whether a recipient can reconstruct the decision, including rejected alternatives and unresolved facts, from the proposed record, an applicable existing export/task format, and a README plus manifest. D-57-3 requires complete agreement on the predeclared fields without author clarification. A round-trip pass establishes record reconstruction only; it supplies no runtime execution, quality ranking, fit verdict, savings or demand result. This defines the next bounded experiment; it is not an assertion that the experiment has run.

Select an existing format or a thin decision-record layer if the baseline reaches parity. Stop the distinct paid-record proposition if a future authorized #63 sample reaches D-57-4's disconfirmation condition. Defer optional stack-level outcomes and any comparative fit/cost claims until their evidence gates are met. Model-first and hardware-first remain narrower alternatives only for a supported source/use subset; they are not fallback claims of validated accuracy.
