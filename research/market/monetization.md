# Monetization and neutral recommendations

Status: research proposal, accessed 2026-10-16.

| Model | Evidence / comparable | Opportunity | Constraint / next test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free discovery plus paid research/data | [BenchLM data access](https://benchlm.ai/data/access) | Subscription for retained history or team work | Data display is separately licensed; access fees do not buy redistribution rights |
| Usage/API facilitation | [OpenRouter pricing](https://openrouter.ai/pricing) | Execution path after comparison | Costs, routing and fees depend on provider and usage; requires explicit economics |
| Cloud or team subscription | [Dify plans](https://dify.ai/pricing), [n8n plans](https://n8n.io/pricing/) | Shared builds, evidence reviews and governance | Willingness to pay not established; avoid charging for generic directory access alone |
| Hardware/cloud referral | [ModelFit disclosure](https://modelfit.io/) | Referral revenue where genuine purchase intent exists | Commission incentive can distort rankings; no partner agreement established |
| Deployment/consulting service | Product hypothesis | Paid integration and verification | Labor and liability make it a service business; not validated by this study |

All monetary rates must be captured in dated pricebooks only when complete units and conditions exist. This initial research compares business-model structure and links live pricing pages; it does not copy vendor rates into an incompatible parallel price catalog. Country, taxes, discounts, storage, egress, idle capacity, support and commitment terms must be evaluated for an actual quote.

Neutrality design proposal: rank adequacy against disclosed user constraints before revenue; show sponsorship separately; publish scoring inputs and allow a commission-free view; never hide a technically superior unpaid option. Log source and ranking changes for review. Compare subscription willingness against referral economics after customer research, not before.
