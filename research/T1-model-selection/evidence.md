## Evidence policy and gaps

Use the grades from `docs/RESEARCH_METHOD.md`: **Primary documentation**, **Vendor claim**, **Independent measurement**, **Estimate**. Apply the [accepted #41 evidence policy](../T0-configuration/catalog-data-sources.md#evidence-grades-and-labels), retaining the [historical #75 owner adoption](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/pull/75#pullrequestreview-5550616927): **checked** means confirmed against the source, while **verified** means independently reproduced. This submission's vendor-backed proposal provenance is `unverified`; the citation/access date still records what was checked. Eligible, version-specific documentation can decide a constraint while its evidence remains unverified. No model-quality measurement was run or account's purchased entitlement established.

The [accepted #41 freshness policy](../T0-configuration/catalog-data-sources.md#freshness) distinguishes pinned facts (recheck on source correction/version change) from dated current-state prices/terms/availability (as-of date, no invented TTL). Resolve apparent conflicts by units, eligibility/scope, version/effective date, then fact-specific authority. A publisher's requirement beats its capability listing; billing/legal beats overview. Equally ranked numeric evidence uses median/spread and the conservative value in the rule's direction, flagged disputed if crossing a threshold. Only no eligible evidence or equally ranked opposite yes/no claims remains unknown and needs reproduction. Qwen config versus card and direct versus Bedrock context must first be scoped, not blended.
