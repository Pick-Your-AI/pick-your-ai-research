Choose the user's actual knowledge sources, the permitted uses of their content, and the path by which source revisions, permissions and deletion events reach every affected consumer. This record supplies an engineering decision and evidence requirements under the user's project delegation. It does not select a customer's dataset, grant content-use rights, exercise a connector or certify a deployment. ### Common evaluation envelope

KB-01–04 are compatibility proposals in research revision `knowledge-base-decision/0.2.0` using the [shared rule format](../../templates/rule-proposal.md). Source-checked mechanisms support scoped facts; the engineering assertions below are not independently verified runtime rules. This revision replaces the earlier compressed acknowledgement-based KB-02 example and makes action/path bindings explicit. It changes no frozen schema, baseline ruleset or test-only implementation.

1. **Validate the whole binding first.** Apply schema, reference and build-wide selection identity/role checks before disabled, empty or Boolean shortcuts. Malformed or contradictory identity is an input error, not a compatibility pass. An unresolved reference or incomplete qualified path/operation inventory remains unresolved. Selected `data_source`/`knowledge_base`/`storage`/`embedding_model`/`vector_database` instances participate where present, together with their actual retrieval/application/tool/framework/memory paths. Do not force a separate `knowledge_base`, `memory_store` or vector product merely to represent a valid declared implementation.
2. **Separate required presence from instance controls.** A declared knowledge/memory requirement needs an evidenced qualifying implementation; an existential witness can satisfy only that requirement. Evaluate the independent rules over every enabled applicable path in the actual scope, including secondary and tool/memory paths. Explicitly absent optional capability can skip its own rules only from a complete qualified inventory. Unknown selection or applicability is not absence. The existing RAG-01–06 consumer gate still requires a bound `rag_pipeline` for its tool path until #40 supplies an explicit versioned actual-path binding; this research proposal supplies no bypass.
3. **Keep answer states and terminal outcomes typed.** Follow the [current CD-1 terminal boundary](../T0-configuration/component-schema-compatibility.md#cd-1-concrete-answer-encoding-and-normalization-experiment-2026-10-18): `pass`/`fail`/`unknown`/`skipped`, fixed severity, responsible input and causal reason remain distinct. Missing, withheld and explicitly unknown evidence cannot become false, zero or a match. Known false applicability skips. A valid caller-qualified not-applicable answer can skip its bounded dependent rule; it cannot waive required presence or certify an undisclosed path's absence. The older Boolean/None sidecars do not preserve this terminal contract.
4. **Qualify evidence before reducing it.** Bind source, authority, selected resource/operation/configuration, version, date and applicable requested scope under [CD-3](../T0-configuration/component-schema-compatibility.md#cd-3-scoped-source-evidence-candidate-2026-10-18). Normalize terms and resolve qualified conflicts conservatively; unknown means missing eligible evidence or an unresolved eligible tie. Use a qualified interval's conservative endpoint for a limit, retaining its uncertainty. Positive support evidence can witness its scoped member; an unmarked or incomplete list omission cannot prove unsupported. ALL can fail on one known requested member's eligible explicit exclusion or absence from an exhaustive applicable support inventory despite unresolved other members. ANY can pass on one eligible witness, while disproving ANY needs the complete qualified requested alternatives accounted for. An empty support list cannot answer an unknown requested set.
5. **Preserve each result and known violations.** With valid identity and known-true applicability, a qualified counterexample to an all-required assertion fails even when other required evidence is unknown. An all-required pass needs complete applicable scope and all required evidence; otherwise retain unknown. Keep per-instance findings; a wholly inapplicable qualified scope skips, while an empty or unresolved inventory cannot silently witness a required capability. Severity and `on_unknown` are fixed per rule. Do not emit a second error merely because the original rule is unknown.

The [CD-13 reader](../T0-configuration/component-schema-compatibility.md#cd-13-typed-selection-binding-proposal-2026-10-18) retains its actual Boolean binding and admission limits; it does not discover these richer source, action or lifecycle facts. A digest binds bytes, not their authority or completeness. #40 must separately version the operands, declaration/binding, caller, rule adapter and migration before executable adoption. This note is neither a new Ruleset nor proof that a consumer has adopted one.

**KB-01 detail.** The trusted application/operator must bind the actual source, principal, tenant/resource scope, allowed action and current policy before content or derived snippets/metadata reach an unauthorized reranker, model, tool, trace/export or other consumer. Content-derived or model-supplied filter arguments cannot grant authority. A public anonymous read with positively established absence of an additional protection requirement may skip that confidentiality check; this neither grants intake/training rights nor skips public-resource administration or mutation. Known unauthorized exposure/action fails; an unexamined mapping or enforcement setting remains unknown. K5/K6 show why ingestion authority and source administration cannot be equated with per-request content permission; K11 describes one separately configured preview mechanism, not universal propagation.

**KB-02 detail.** Bind the authorized event, source/revision and affected principal/action/resource scope to unambiguous current destination/generation receipts with observation time, method and effective configuration. Revoking one principal's access does not assert that content must be destroyed for every still-authorized principal. A tombstone qualifies only when evidence establishes actual serving prevention for the affected scope; merely writing a marker or receiving an acknowledgement does not. An old-event receipt, sampled disappearance or incomplete alternate-path inventory leaves complete exclusion unknown. A known applicable stale serving copy fails even if another layer is unobserved. A complete positively evidenced empty affected serving scope can pass this predicate; an empty list alone cannot.

Maintain separate inventories for serving exclusion and the whole retention/deletion policy. Include the source, staging/parser outputs, chunks, vector fields and old generations, graphs/summaries, memory/history/checkpoints, caches, provider copies and backups that actually exist; do not fabricate missing layers or assume a generic list is complete. A backup with evidenced current non-serving status may coexist with a KB-02 serving pass, while its retained/pending state still prevents a whole-policy completion claim when that policy requires its completion. Restore, replay and re-ingestion must reconcile current revocation before exposing affected content. **KB-03 detail.** Receive #14's [EMB-01–04](rules/embedding-rules.md) using the actual document-encoder role/profile, requested dimension, tokenizer/processor revision and preprocessing instructions. Record the declared transform's retained/lost content and parsing failures; if intentional lossy intake is allowed, that policy and its evaluation remain explicit rather than being described as complete source preservation. The actual complete embedding payload still must fit every applicable effective bound. Query and document instructions may differ when the selected model requires that distinction; equal width alone never proves a compatible generation. ### Refresh, storage and operational evidence requirements

These are project requirements for a selected configuration, not measurements or additional universal vendor capabilities. Preserve a source/revision or checkpoint boundary, exact processed scope, partial failures and a replay/idempotency policy. A successful request or job cannot certify complete processing, current source authorization, whole serving visibility or completed deletion. K3 explicitly distinguishes sync completion from later query visibility for affected stores; K2 shows that change detection and deletion detection need different configuration.

Bind every parser/chunker change and re-indexing generation to its source lineage before replacing a serving view. Quarantine or explicitly account for failed extraction and omitted fields; do not let a partial result imply an exhaustive authorized corpus. K4 documents discarded metadata when a destination field is absent, and K8/K10 describe optional parser/intermediate outputs that require inclusion when that path is selected. These examples motivate the inventory requirement; they do not prove a particular deployment contains such outputs.

Choose source-specific freshness and stale-serving policies from #2's requirements and the actual source/action risk. Record configured schedule separately from observed lag and permission-revocation handling; no fixed TTL, latency, revocation deadline or legal retention period is invented. Storage region, operator, encryption/key lifecycle, retention, backup and restore facts must match the actual #9 offering and each selected copy. MEM-SG04/05 observation limits remain intact: unknown is not zero, a sampled read-back is bounded, and private content/subject capture is not enabled by this decision.

For cost intake, preserve full versus incremental processing, the actual transform/embedding events, old/new generation storage and retained backups separately. K3's metadata-only optimization is conditional and still writes the merged vectors; K8's selected parser may have a separate meter. This note supplies no rates, conversions, free-tier claim, invoice or workload total. #39 qualifies actual event quantities and selected offering/region/currency/date before pricing; source access and permitted data use remain independent.

### Permission and lifecycle implications

**Azure preview boundary [K11].** The RBAC-scope ingestion page identifies indexer support from `2025-05-01-preview`; its current examples use `2026-08-01-preview`. It requires managed-identity configuration, permission metadata/index fields and query-time caller identity through `x-ms-query-source-authorization`. Container RBAC inheritance is not Azure ABAC support. For indexer-based ingestion the page requires default parsing mode and `indexerPermissionOptions` containing `rbacScope`. This capability cannot be generalized to arbitrary parsers, legacy API versions or skill pipelines. KB-01 remains unknown for an unexamined configuration; a metadata field alone is not enforcement.

**Bedrock role boundary [K6].** AWS says `CreateDataSource`, `UpdateDataSource` and `StartIngestionJob` do not pass a role for an `iam:PassRole` check: ingestion uses the role already associated with the KB. Someone managing sources can ingest locations that role can read without separately holding S3 permissions. Therefore the proposed acceptance check must examine data-source administration, role bucket/prefix scope and retrieval access together. An inclusion prefix is not a replacement for IAM scope. K5 also requires the S3 bucket and KB to share a Region and limits this connector to general-purpose buckets.

**Chunk and sync boundary [K9, K10, K3].** The API disallows changing `chunkingConfiguration` after creating the connector. No-chunking treats a document as one chunk and loses page-number citations/filtering. These text strategies do not define multimodal chunking. Metadata-only resync avoids embedding calls only under documented conditions: content unchanged, non-CSV content and no custom transformation Lambda. CSV metadata changes require re-ingestion. Record that condition when estimating #39 re-embedding costs; do not assume every metadata update is free or every update re-embeds.
