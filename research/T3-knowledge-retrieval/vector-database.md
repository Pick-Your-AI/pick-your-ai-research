| Directly assembled knowledge / long context | The authorized, complete content required for this request is available to its declared assembly path without vector retrieval | CTX-01 evaluates the complete serialized request against the exact model/route limits after the generation tokenizer/template and any permitted transformation. | Application/framework or hosted retrieval path | Its actual implementation supplies the required operation and backing evidence without an independently selected vector product | No forced `vector_database`, `rag_pipeline` or `memory_store` selection is inferred from a framework name. ## Proposed compatibility rules

### Common evaluation envelope

The four VDB proposals are compatibility rules in research revision `vector-database-decision/0.2.0`. Source facts can support a scoped assertion; these engineering policies are not independently verified runtime rules. Participants are the actual vector path and its `vector_database`/`embedding_model` selections where present, connected retrieval/application/framework and optional memory implementations. `rag_pipeline`, `agent_framework` or `memory_store` presence is not required merely to discover a real path. Its binding remains a concrete #40 integration requirement, not something frozen v1 already evaluates.

1. **Validate identity and scope first.** Reject malformed, duplicate or contradictory binding records before truth shortcuts. Preserve unresolved references or incomplete selection/path/operation inventory as unresolved; a primary selection or unrelated valid row cannot witness another path. Evaluate relevant checks for every enabled selected path/operation in the qualified scope, including secondary/tool/memory paths. A required capability's existence may be satisfied by one evidenced implementation, while all enabled applicable instances still retain their independent controls.
2. **Keep answer states and policy typed.** Use the [CD-1 terminal boundary](../T0-configuration/component-schema-compatibility.md#cd-1-concrete-answer-encoding-and-normalization-experiment-2026-10-18) for any eventual state-aware adapter: `pass`/`fail`/`unknown`/`skipped`, fixed severity and actual causal reason are separate. Missing, withheld and explicitly unknown evidence cannot become false, zero or a successful match. A valid, caller-qualified not-applicable input may skip its bounded dependent rule; it never certifies that an undisclosed path is absent or waives required presence. The older Boolean/None sidecar adapters cannot preserve this terminal contract.
3. **Qualify evidence, then evaluate.** Follow #41 normalization, exact scope, version/date, authority and conservative tie resolution; use unknown for missing eligible evidence or a genuine unresolved tie. A valid interval crossing a limit uses its conservative endpoint and retains uncertainty. For support sets, explicit applicable membership can witness support; an omission from an unmarked/incomplete list cannot prove unsupported. An ALL assertion can fail on one known requested member's eligible explicit exclusion or absence from a qualified exhaustive applicable support inventory, even when other requested membership is unresolved. For ANY, one eligible positive witness can suffice; disproving ANY requires the complete eligible requested alternatives to be accounted for and unsupported within the qualified scope. A complete empty support list cannot answer an unknown requested set.
4. **Preserve known violations.** With valid identity and known-true applicability, a known counterexample to an all-required assertion fails even if another required item is unknown. An all-required pass needs complete qualified scope and all required evidence; unknown scope prevents a vacuous pass. For existential alternatives, a qualified positive witness can satisfy only that requirement; disproving it needs the complete eligible alternatives. Missing evidence is a reason on the concerned rule, not a duplicate error rule. Severity and `on_unknown` do not change with the outcome.

The [current CD-3 source/support experiments](../T0-configuration/component-schema-compatibility.md#cd-3-scoped-source-evidence-candidate-2026-10-18) retain their own versions and admission limits. This note neither adopts their output as a released ruleset nor executes them against vendor evidence. A future adapter must explicitly bind the selected caller, rule, evidence and complete inventories; no conversion from a document-level capability boolean is accepted by implication.

| ID | Applicability / assertion | Severity / unknown policy | Synthetic cases and remediation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VDB-01 | A vector-producing/index/query path is enabled: its actual representation, dtype and shape fit every applicable limit of the exact selected backend/release/index/field configuration | `error` / `review` | Dense 1,024 under an eligible 2,000 bound passes this capacity assertion; 3,072 exceeds it and fails only that index/type; missing applicable bound is unknown; an evidenced vector-free path is skipped. Choose an evidenced compatible type/index or approved encoder reduction |
| VDB-02 | Any protected read, or any write/delete/administrative operation, on a selected store: bind the authenticated principal to the trusted resource/namespace/row/payload scope **and** require that principal's permission for the requested action on that resource | `error` / `block` | Authorized principal, scope and action pass; a reader attempting delete fails even in the correct tenant; caller-controlled unvalidated tenant key fails; missing scope/action-policy evidence is unknown. Public anonymous read-only retrieval with no confidentiality requirement is skipped, but public-store mutations/administration still evaluate. Enforce action authorization as well as data scoping; indexing alone grants neither |
| VDB-03 | An authorized deletion/revocation event affects a vector-serving path: no affected source-derived generation remains eligible for serving across its complete applicable serving inventory | `error` / `block` | Known current stale serving content fails even with unrelated unknown layers. A complete event/configuration-bound exclusion record passes serving only; acknowledgement, incomplete lineage or a bounded sample alone leaves complete exclusion unknown. Known no applicable event skips. Reconcile with KB-02 and M6 rather than invent another erasure policy |
| VDB-04 | A declared quality requirement relies on ANN/hybrid retrieval: its evaluation evidence matches the actual corpus/query distribution, index, filter, scoring/fusion and required protocol/metrics | `warning` / `review` | Matching eligible coverage passes this evidence-comparability check; known incompatible configuration fails; absent eligible evidence or incomplete required metric coverage is unknown; an explicitly absent quality claim skips. #36 separately judges the measured result against the target |

**VDB-01 bindings and remediation.** Use the actual named field and effective dtype/index configuration, not a product-wide maximum or another primary database. EMB-01 independently checks encoder-output/field-width equality; EMB-02 binds query/document generation compatibility; EMB-03 retains normalization, operator, sign and ordering. Dense width is not sparse nonzero count. A documented reduction is a new representation decision, not permission to truncate silently. Synthetic 3,072-dimensional output exceeds the cited pgvector HNSW `vector` bound; an eligible `halfvec` path can satisfy capacity while precision, generation compatibility and workload quality remain separate.

**VDB-02 bindings and remediation.** Evaluate the actual principal/action/resource and effective policy revision before exposure or side effects, including mutations to a public store. A namespace, matching tenant key, credential reference, payload index, successful transport or `supports_acl` flag cannot supply authority. An explicitly public, unprotected read may skip this confidentiality check only for that read; a missing classification/policy is unknown. Reject evidenced unauthorized action or untrusted scope construction, and obtain trusted action enforcement through #35/RAG-02 rather than treating a filter as a grant.

**VDB-03 bindings and remediation.** Bind the authorized event, source/revision and derived record generations to every relevant serving destination/configuration, with the verification time/method and inventory completeness. Recheck fetch, query, cache, alternate index and restore-to-serving paths that can expose affected content. A deletion acknowledgement proves only its documented acknowledgement scope. A tombstone or access filter can establish serving exclusion only with eligible enforcement and complete affected-scope evidence; it does not prove physical removal. Known stale serving data remains failure. Restore or re-ingestion must reconcile the event before admitting affected content for serving again. The lifecycle section below separates backup retention and whole-policy completion.

**VDB-04 bindings and remediation.** Record the actual dataset/source rights, corpus and query revisions, relevance protocol, authorization filters, ANN effort, scoring/fusion/reranker configuration and all required metrics/conditions. A vendor benchmark, connector constructor or differently filtered corpus cannot qualify the selected workload by itself. A comparable record may report inadequate quality: passing VDB-04 does not override the #36 quality target. Obtain eligible evaluation evidence under its authorized method; this decision executes none.

## Security and lifecycle findings

Qdrant's `is_tenant` option optimizes storage layout; its documentation separately applies tenant filters [V3]. A shard or payload index is not an access grant. Pinecone namespace selection also needs trusted application binding [V4]. PostgreSQL row-level security is not enabled by default; table owners normally bypass it unless forced, superusers/`BYPASSRLS` roles bypass it, and `TRUNCATE`/`REFERENCES` are outside row-policy scope [V6]. These source observations motivate VDB-02; none proves a deployed configuration is secure.

A vector-backed memory path may reuse a suitable database selection, with its own evidenced index/namespace, representation and authorized operation binding. It need not reuse the retrieval index, and a valid application/framework memory implementation need not select a `memory_store`. Preserve source/chunk/representation generations and policy lineage so #13 can trace affected derivations and #14 can plan re-indexing. Product identity, backing label or available primary storage establishes neither actual persistence/recovery nor #28 purpose, consent or action authority.

| Evidence dimension | What it can establish at its exact event/configuration scope | What remains separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request/acknowledgement or tombstone | The recorded command acceptance or marker state | Actual serving enforcement, coverage and physical removal |
| Current serving exclusion | A complete qualified record that affected content cannot be served on the relevant paths | Non-serving history, checkpoints, summaries/graph, provider copies, backups and full deletion |
| Retained non-serving layer | Actual state, access restrictions and declared expiry/deletion condition | Retention is not automatically a violation, nor proof of erasure; apply the selected policy and current evidence |
| Whole-policy deletion completion | Complete event-bound inventory plus evidence that every required layer/condition meets the selected #28/#35 policy | A serving pass, sampled readback or elapsed planned expiry cannot certify this alone |
| Restore/re-ingestion | An actual recovery path that reconciles revocation before exposure | A prior serving pass does not automatically cover a new generation or restored copy |

This is the accepted [M1–M8 memory interface](../T4-agents-integrations/agent-memory.md#current-shared-format-rule-dispositions-2026-10-18), [KB-02](knowledge-base.md) and #35 `G-H-MEM-01` boundary applied to vector storage. Do not invent a universal TTL, purge method, legal-consent requirement or backup deadline. Actual layer states and any required observations use [MEM-SG04/05](../T6-deployment-operations/memory-observability-proposal.md) with their event, sampling, access and content-capture limits. The database/service's documented capability remains distinct from tested isolation, completed deletion or a customer-specific permission.
